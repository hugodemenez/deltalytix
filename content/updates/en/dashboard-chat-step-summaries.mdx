# Clearer steps in the dashboard chat

When the dashboard chat looks through your data, its work now collapses into a single line such as **Analyzed in 4 steps**. It stays open while the answer is being written and closes when it's done. Open it to see each lookup (recent trades, weekly summary, and so on) and what it returned.

When the assistant needs your go-ahead, the question appears inline with **Approve** and **Deny**, then turns into a short "Approved" or "Denied" line.
