# Jump to a month or year, and filter news from one chip

The calendar widget on [the dashboard](/en/dashboard) no longer uses a static **August 2026** title. In daily view, **Month** and **Year** chips sit in the header, with prev/next around the month. Weekly view keeps a **Year** chip; the chevrons step years.

![Calendar header with August and 2026 chips, monthly total, and a News chip](/updates/pr-498/en/calendar-header-month-year-news.png)

Open **News** (newspaper icon; icon-only on a narrow screen) for country search and importance — **Low**, **Medium**, **High**. The menu reuses **Filter by Country**, **All Countries**, and **No countries found**. A badge on the chip shows how many countries you selected. The year list runs from 2000 through the current year plus two; a year you are already on outside that range still appears. Open month and year lists stay dark text on white in both themes, so Edge dark mode no longer paints a blank overlay. This is a follow-up to [events already on the calendar](/en/updates/calendar-events-and-mobile-details), not a new events feed.

On [the dashboard](/en/dashboard) in **Widgets**, use **Month** / **Year**, then open **News** and pick a country or importance.
