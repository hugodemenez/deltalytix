# Widgets, Table, and Accounts are centered tabs on desktop again

On a wide window, [the dashboard](/en/dashboard) again shows **Widgets**, **Table**, and **Accounts** as tabs — centered in the top bar, over the filters and share row, not as the old full-width strip under the navbar. Arrow keys, Home, and End move between them. This revises the [v5 view menu](/en/updates/dashboard-v5-shell); the three views and their data are unchanged.

![Desktop dashboard navbar with centered Widgets, Table, and Accounts tabs, Widgets selected](/updates/pr-498/en/dashboard-centered-view-tabs.png)

On a phone or a narrow window, the same views stay in a compact dropdown that shows the current view name and a chevron. The switcher is home chrome only — it does not appear on Connections, Data, Settings, or Billing.

Open [the dashboard](/en/dashboard) on a wide window and use the centered tabs; narrow the window to see the compact view dropdown.
