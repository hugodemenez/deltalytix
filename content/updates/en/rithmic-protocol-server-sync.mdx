# Server-side synchronization for Rithmic R | Protocol

Traders with approved Rithmic R | Protocol access now have a new server-side route from their order and fill history into Deltalytix.

From [Connections](/en/dashboard/connections), choose **Add Rithmic Protocol connection**, select the Rithmic system available to your deployment, and enter your credentials. Deltalytix discovers the hosted accounts you can access, lets you choose which ones to connect, and converts synchronized fills into journal trades.

This integration is distinct from—and does not replace—the existing Rithmic synchronization workflow. Availability requires a configured Protocol endpoint plus valid credentials and R | Protocol entitlements from your broker or prop firm; not every Rithmic account is eligible. System choices can also vary by deployment.

If your account is entitled, [open Connections](/en/dashboard/connections) to add **Rithmic Protocol** and start synchronization.
