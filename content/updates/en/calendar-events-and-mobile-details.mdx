# Financial events return to a mobile-ready calendar

The dashboard calendar once again places scheduled financial events alongside your trading activity, restoring market context after the previous event feed stopped working.

Calendar review is also easier on a phone. Opening a day or week on a small screen now reveals a height-constrained bottom drawer with scrollable trades and events. Larger screens keep the richer dialog presentation.

[Open the dashboard calendar](/en/dashboard) on a narrow screen and select a day or week to review its details. Event coverage depends on the available data for the selected period.
