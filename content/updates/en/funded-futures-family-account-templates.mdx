# Funded Futures Family account templates

[Account configuration](/en/dashboard) now includes **Funded Futures Family** in the **Load Template** list, alongside firms such as Apex and My Funded Futures. Search **FFF**, **FFFamily**, or **Family**, pick the Prime, Velocity, Premier+, or Straight-to-Funded size that matches the account, and the drawdown, profit target, consistency, and payout-day fields fill in from the public plan rules.

This is a different firm from **My Funded Futures** and from Funded Futures Network. If you already synced a Tradovate FFF account, open that account's configuration and load the matching template instead of leaving the prop firm blank.
