# Funded Futures Family account templates

**Funded Futures Family** is now in **Load Template** on [account configuration](/en/dashboard). Search for the firm or a plan name such as Velocity, Prime, Premier+, S2F, or Base, pick the size that matches your Tradovate account, and the drawdown, profit target, consistency, and payout fields fill in from the published FFF rules.

This is a different firm from **My Funded Futures**. Evaluation, funded, FastPass, Daily Add-On, and Straight-to-Funded Accelerate variants are listed separately so you can match the account you actually trade.
