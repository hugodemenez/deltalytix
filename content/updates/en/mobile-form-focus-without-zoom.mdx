# Mobile forms stay at the scale you chose

Focusing a compact dashboard field on iPhone no longer magnifies the whole page and leaves it enlarged after editing. Text fields, text areas, searches, selectors, comments, and inline trade edits now use an iOS-safe text size on narrow screens, while retaining their compact layout on larger displays.
