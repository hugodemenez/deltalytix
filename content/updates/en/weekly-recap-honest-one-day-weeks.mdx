# Weekly recap names one-day weeks as one session

In the Sunday [weekly trading recap](/en/updates/weekly-trading-recap-email), a green week with only one session is now named as one session — not a ramp that never happened. The paragraph under **Net P&L** no longer says the week “started soft” or “ramped up” from a single day of P&L. Day-to-day or “shape of the week” language appears only when at least two days have real P&L variation.

The later coaching sentence, above **Book a call\***, stays tied to that data: one day is not a week-long trend. It may still name a Deltalytix tool when that fits. The subject stays *Your trading statistics for the week 📈*. The disclaimer is unchanged: **This recap is generated automatically and may contain errors.**

Keep the weekly recap preference on. After a green Monday–Sunday UTC week — including a week with only one session — read the paragraph under **Net P&L** in the Sunday mail. **Unsubscribe** remains in the footer.
