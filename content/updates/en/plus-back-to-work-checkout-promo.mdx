# Back to Work prices on Plus, applied at checkout

New **Plus** checkouts for **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, and **Yearly** (USD or EUR) apply the **Back to Work** promotion without typing a code. [Pricing](/en/pricing) and the homepage pricing section show a **Back to Work** note that the discount is applied automatically at checkout, and the Plus card can show a struck regular price next to the offer amount when Stripe returns the coupon. [Billing](/en/dashboard/billing) uses the same **Back to Work** pill on those periods.

**Lifetime** Plus is not part of this campaign. Basic is unchanged. The offer only appears when the matching Stripe promotion codes are configured and still active; if they are not, checkout keeps the usual field for a typed promo code.

Open [pricing](/en/pricing) or [Billing](/en/dashboard/billing), choose monthly, quarterly, or yearly Plus, and start checkout while the campaign is on.
