# Widget explanations that work on touch screens

Information buttons across dashboard and embedded widgets now open accessible popovers instead of hover-dependent tooltips. Explanations for charts, statistics, accounts, filters, mindset, and trade tables are therefore reachable on phones without being clipped off-screen.

Open a popover with a tap, click, or keyboard focus. It adjusts to the available viewport, closes with **Escape** or an outside press, and respects reduced-motion preferences.

[Open the dashboard](/en/dashboard) on a phone or narrow window and tap an information icon to try it.
