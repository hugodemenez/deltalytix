# Compare Deltalytix to Trademetria, TradeZella, and Tradervue

Visitors choosing a futures journal can now compare Deltalytix to Trademetria, TradeZella, and Tradervue from [the futures journal comparison](/en/trading-journal/futures). The hub and the three matchups are live in English and French. This is a public marketing surface, not a dashboard change.

The heading is **One trading journal for every futures account.**, the same line from [the English homepage trading journal update](/en/updates/en-trading-journal-positioning).

## The hub

The page opens with the eyebrow **Trading journal · Futures**, that heading, the lede **Import your brokers and funded accounts, then read P&L in one place.**, and the **Get Started** and **See pricing** actions.

Under **Journals comparison**, Deltalytix stays first, marked **Us** and **You’re here** — that row has no **View more →**. Trademetria, TradeZella, and Tradervue each have a one-liner and **View more →**. Only live journals appear in the table.

![Journals comparison table with Deltalytix marked Us and You’re here, and View more links for Trademetria, TradeZella, and Tradervue](/updates/pr-488/en/compare-hub-journals-table.png)

The page closes with **Start free, forever.**

## Side-by-side pages

**View more →** opens a 1:1 page titled **Deltalytix vs {name}.**, with a breadcrumb **Trading journal** → **Futures**. Each page walks through sections that put Deltalytix next to the other journal — for example **WHAT YOU GET**.

![Deltalytix vs TradeZella page with breadcrumb, Get Started, and the WHAT YOU GET section](/updates/pr-488/en/compare-tradezella-what-you-get.png)

## Finding it

The homepage footer adds a **Journals comparison** column: **All journals**, **vs Trademetria**, **vs TradeZella**, and **vs Tradervue**. Opening [Trading journal](/en/trading-journal) permanently redirects to the hub; it is not a separate landing.

Open [the futures journal comparison](/en/trading-journal/futures), then **View more →** on [TradeZella](/en/trading-journal/futures/tradezella) or [Tradervue](/en/trading-journal/futures/tradervue). The same hub is reachable from **Journals comparison** on [the homepage](/en).
