# DxFeed connect detects your prop firm from your login

Connecting through DxFeed is a single-step form now. From [the dashboard](/en/dashboard) connections strip or [Connections](/en/dashboard/connections), add **DxFeed**. The **Add DxFeed connection** sheet asks for one thing: **Sign in with the same username and password you use on your prop firm's DxFeed platform. We detect the firm from that login.**

![Add DxFeed connection sheet with empty Username and Password fields and no prop-firm picker](/updates/pr-475/en/dxfeed-single-step-form.png)

The separate prop-firm picker step is gone — there's no list to search and no **Continue** step before credentials. The username field also accepts non-email logins (for example `trader_ht50`), which some firms issue instead of an email address; validation just checks that a username and a password were entered.

Behind the scenes, the firm is detected from your DxFeed/Volumetrica authentication host and its reported firm name, with a fallback to a generic "DxFeed" label when a firm can't be matched to a known name. There's still no allowlist: using DxFeed doesn't guarantee every prop firm's data is available, and connecting still requires a real provider endpoint to complete.

[Add a DxFeed connection](/en/dashboard/connections) to see the single-step form.
