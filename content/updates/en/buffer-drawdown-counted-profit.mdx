# Drawdown on buffered accounts now follows counted profit

On accounts with a buffer, the balance only counts profit above the buffer, but drawdown was still measured on full equity. That could show a trailing account as breached right after it crossed the buffer, or hide a real breach on a static account after a dip back through it.

Drawdown now uses the same above-buffer curve as the balance, so the trailing floor and remaining loss match what the account actually counts. Accounts without a buffer are unchanged.
