# Account payment reminder names the firm and the day

The scheduled **Account payment** reminder now leads with when the firm is due: **{Firm} payment in {n} days.** (or **{Firm} payment tomorrow.**). It is the prop-firm account notice — not the weekly recap, not Plus billing. The greeting uses your newsletter first name, or **Trader** if that name is missing or looks like an email.

A mint calendar for the month of today marks today as a filled circle, the payment day as an outlined circle, and the days between with a wash. Weekdays stay **M T W T F S S**. The lede is the locked monthly line — **Auto-renew monthly is set at the firm.** — it does not change for quarterly or annual accounts. The caption is **{n} days left** (or **1 day left**).

**Change reminder** and **Turn off this notice** both open [the dashboard](/en/dashboard). Unsubscribe still goes to [notification settings](/en/settings/notifications). Mail comes from Deltalytix Renewals at `renewals@eu.updates.deltalytix.app`. This follows [account emails that were localized and no longer empty](/en/updates/clearer-localized-account-emails); **Renewal Notice Days** on the account is unchanged.

On [Accounts](/en/dashboard), keep **Renewal Notice Days** set. When the next payment date enters that window, the new letter arrives — one email per account.
