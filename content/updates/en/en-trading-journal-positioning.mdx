# The English homepage now leads with "trading journal"

[The English homepage](/en) hero now reads **One trading journal for every futures account.**, with the subhead **Import your brokers and funded accounts, then read P&L in one place.** The document title search engines and browser tabs show is now **Deltalytix — The Trading Journal for Futures Traders**, and the same "trading journal" and "futures" framing carries into the meta description and the OG/Twitter card headline, subhead, and alt text used for social link previews.

![English homepage hero with the trading journal headline and Get Started action](/updates/pr-475/en/en-hero-trading-journal.png)

This is a copy change to the EN hero and crawl/social metadata only — the visible OG image layout is unchanged, and the French homepage and its metadata are unaffected.

[Read the English homepage](/en) to see the new hero copy.
