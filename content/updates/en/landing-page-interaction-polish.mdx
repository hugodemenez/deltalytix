# A lighter, quicker tour through the homepage

The Deltalytix homepage now moves more naturally from the opening message into the product. Use **Features ↓** to smoothly reach the feature overview—the address updates to `#features`, so the destination is easy to revisit or share.

</video>

The AI coach preview is now a compact, text-only conversation with tighter spacing between turns. Performance previews advance every three seconds, while pause and manual navigation remain available. If you prefer reduced motion, the carousel still leaves its progress animation disabled.

![Compact text-only AI coach conversation](/updates/pr-439/en/landing-ai-coach-compact.png)

![Homepage performance preview with carousel controls](/updates/pr-439/en/landing-performance-preview.png)

Partner logos also appear without faint boxes around them, keeping the closing section focused on the brands themselves. [Explore the updated homepage](/en) to see the complete flow.
