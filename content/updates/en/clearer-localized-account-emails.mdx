# Account emails in the right language, with complete content

Email sign-in messages can now follow the user's selected English or French locale, making authentication instructions easier to understand. Subscription-renewal notices also include their intended message instead of arriving with an empty body.

This focused update applies to authentication and renewal emails; it does not change every transactional email. Request a new email sign-in link to receive the localized template.
