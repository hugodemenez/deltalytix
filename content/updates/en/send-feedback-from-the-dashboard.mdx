# Send feedback without leaving the dashboard

A new **Send feedback or report a bug** action in the dashboard navbar gives you a direct line to the Deltalytix team without interrupting your work.

![Feedback form opened from the dashboard navbar](/updates/pr-378/en/dashboard-feedback-popover.png)

Open **Share your feedback**, choose a category, and enter the required message. After submission, an on-screen confirmation appears and Deltalytix sends an acknowledgement to your signed-in account email.

[Open the dashboard](/en/dashboard) and select the message icon in the navbar to share an idea or report a problem.
